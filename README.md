# Classifying beehives states through acoustic analysis

## Why we need to detect Queenless & Infested???
A Queen bee lays an average of 1,200 - 2,000 eggs per day. If the queen is weak or disappears, the number of worker bees will decrease rapidly. If signs of swarm dispersal are detected (By Queenless) farmers can create a new beehive before half of the bees migrate with the old queen.

## What about a infested (Varroa)
The Varroa is a tiny parasite, It's the worst nightmare of beekeepers worldwide, Varroa attached itself to bees and their larvae, feeding on their fatty tissues, The female mite burrows into a bee larvae cell, seals it, lays eggs, and then her offspring feed on the developing larvae
